| Notes

| 1 Heavy is better for &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health than Titanic, but debuffs some of your stats.. It is instead recommended to use Reinforced, which keeps Heavy's high &#xe008;&#160;Defense but without any debuffs, with an easy-to-get reforge stone even for ironman players (see below). Likewise for equipment, Hefty provides more &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health with its high &#xe008;&#160;Defense bonus, but its debuffs makes it typically worse than Colossal at an early-game stage.
2 At Common and Uncommon rarities, Stained gives the same &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance as other reforges, such as Menacing, Soft, Honored, and Blended, all of which are better at low rarities.

3 Legendary produces higher &#xe050;&#160;Damage. However, Sharp gives more &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance, which is often more valuable until you get access to reforge stones.
