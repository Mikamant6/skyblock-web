Currently, in Hypixel SkyBlock, there are 6 types of Slayers: Zombie, Spider, Wolf, Enderman, Blaze, and Vampire. Each type of Slayer has their own unique strategies pertaining to each boss: this guide will detail useful strategies and gear progression for each specific Slayer.

NOTE: The gear outlined below are simply recommended prerequisites, and strictly following this exact progression is not mandatory.

Zombie Slayer involves killing [A sprite icon representing Zombie in Hypixel SkyBlock] Zombies and culminates in killing the [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror. It is widely considered to be the easiest Slayer, due to its low stats and predictable actions: however, inexperienced or weak players may struggle with defeating one.

## Strategies
[edit | edit source]

### Tier I
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;I [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror is relatively easy to kill due to its low &#xe010;&#160;Health and &#xe050;&#160;Damage. Its only ability, Life Drain, drains the player's &#xe010;&#160;Health every 2.5s. Not a lot of gear is required to defeat a Tier&#160;I Revenant Horror as it is barely stronger than a basic [A sprite icon representing Graveyard Zombie in Hypixel SkyBlock] Graveyard Zombie.

### Tier II
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;II [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror, while still relatively easy, is a decent step up from the Tier&#160;I variant. As you progress through Zombie Slayer, you will notice that the Revenant Horror's &#xe050;&#160;Damage is much, much lower than its &#xe010;&#160;Health: as a result, you can focus more on dealing damage.

The Revenant Horror, at Tier&#160;II, gains the Pestilence ability, which deals AoE damage. As a result, one should also prioritise efficient healing through items such as the &#160;Wand of Healing and &#160;Wand of Mending.

### Tier III
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;III [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror is a significant increase in difficulty when considering the previous tiers. At Tier&#160;III, the Revenant Horror gains the Enrage ability: every 40s, the Revenant Horror will increase its attack range by +50% and increase damage dealt from Pestilence by +500%.

It is imperative for players to get their damage up significantly, since killing the Revenant Horror quickly allows you to avoid its Enrage phase.

While the Tier&#160;IV Revenant Horror's damage may not appear as a considerable increase from its Tier&#160;III variant, this is increased by both Life Drain, Pestilence, and Enrage.

As is the case with previous tiers of the Revenant Horror, the best strategy would be to maintain a balance of dealing high damage and healing.

### Tier V
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;V [A sprite icon representing Atoned Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Atoned Horror is completely unique from the [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror, both possessing completely unique abilities and having significantly more &#xe010;&#160;Health and &#xe050;&#160;Damage.

- Redemption allows the Atoned Horror to heal rapidly.

- Explosive Assault allows the Atoned Horror to throw explosive TNT at the player. A radius appears under the area where the TNT will land: damage dealt is dependent on the distance of the player to the center. This attack does a deceptively high amount of damage, so prioritize avoiding it even if it means dealing less damage to the boss unless it is on low health.

- Thermonuclear causes the Atoned Horror to stand still as it charges up, releasing a volley of lightning within a 7 block radius, damaging all players within depending on how many times they have been damaged. This damage will almost always instantly kill you, so do not make the mistake of trying to tank the attack.

- Note that this ability will only be used after the boss has been alive for around 30 seconds, so you can ignore it if you can consistently kill the Atoned Horror before that time.

- The boss is also immune to most &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage, meaning mage items such as the &#160;Ink Wand or &#160;Hyperion are mostly useless against it in terms of their abilities.

To kill the Atoned Horror, a popular strategy is to hold down ⇧ Shift+S and repeatedly hit the boss in order to defeat it. By sneaking, you can avoid moving too fast to the point where you can no longer hit the boss while still avoiding the TNT. This strategy works best with around 370+&#160;&#xe022;&#160;Speed.

While the Atoned Horror is charging up its Thermonuclear attack, it can still be damaged. As a result, if its &#xe010;&#160;Health is low enough, you can kill it while it is charging up and avoid the lightning.

Spider Slayer involves killing [A sprite icon representing Spider in Hypixel SkyBlock] Spiders and culminates in killing the [A sprite icon representing Tarantula Broodfather in Hypixel SkyBlock] Tarantula Broodfather. A step up in difficulty from the [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror, it revolves around anti-healing and dealing heavy damage. It is unlocked after beating a Tier II [A sprite icon representing Revenant Horror in Hypixel SkyBlock] Revenant Horror.

## Strategies
[edit | edit source]

### Tier I
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;I [A sprite icon representing Tarantula Broodfather in Hypixel SkyBlock] Tarantula Broodfather is relatively easy to kill due to its low &#xe010;&#160;Health and&#xe050;&#160;Damage. It's only ability, Backstab, means that it does 50% more &#xe050;&#160;Damage to the player when behind the player. However this can be countered by backing into a wall so that the Tarantula Broodfather can't get behind the player.

Vampire Slayer involves killing Vampires and culminates in killing the [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend. As it is part of The Rift, it features a unique gear progression and can be tackled at essentially any part of the game. For Ironman however, it is recommended to wait until Ghast Tear&#160;V (5) to acquire &#160;12&#160;Silver Fang, which will be required for an important upgrade.

You can preemptively (or later) bring &#160;76&#160;Silver Fang, &#160;Maddox Batphone, &#160;Leggings of the Pack, &#160;16&#160;Re-heated Gummy Polar Bear, &#160;Scarf's Studies and &#160;2&#160;Weird Tuba. Those will be required for some crafts later.

## Strategies
[edit | edit source]

### Tier I
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;I [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend relatively easy to kill due to its low &#xe010;&#160;Health and &#xe01f;&#160;Hearts damage. Coagulation passively deals 0.5&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts of damage every second and be easily staved off with the &#160;Healing Melon. Once it reach 50%&#160;&#xe010;&#160;Health, Mania will have you stand in the highlighted area in green to avoid taking 2&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts of damage. 

| Slot
| Name
| Notes

| [Healing Melon] 

| &#160;Healing Melon

| The &#160;Healing Melon is an important item throughout all of Vampire Slayer, as Coagulation will have you take constant, unavoidable damage. Make sure to only use it when you can heal all 7&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts it provides, as recklessly using the melon early and too much will have you run out of &#xe003;&#160;Mana.

### Tier II
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;II [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend introduce Twinclaw, which deals 3&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts of damage every 7 seconds and cannot be avoided at close range. You will need to use the &#160;Holy Ice to greatly reduce it's damage. Later on, it will use Stonewrath, which is a move in 3 parts: first, it summons 3 Clotgoyles. While alive, they each reduce your max &#xe01f;&#160;Hearts by 1 and can hit you for 10&#160;&#xe020;&#160;Rift Time and 1&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts. When killed, they become a Cadaver. Afterward, the boss will fly up and prepare charging an attack targetted onto you. After 2 second, the location will be locked in, which you must move out of, it will then crash down and deal damage and destroy any Cadaver caught in the explosion. If any Cadaver are left, they will then be ressurected, taking away your maximum &#xe01f;&#160;Hearts again.

Coagulation and Mania now deal 1&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts and 3&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts

### Tier III
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;III [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend introduce Impel and Killer Spring, it will now use Mania at 75%&#160;&#xe010;&#160;Health and 40%&#160;&#xe010;&#160;Health instead. During Mania, Impel will require you to perform a small task (one of the following: CLICK UP, CLICK DOWN, JUMP, SNEAK) within 2s. Failing to complete the task will deal 4&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts. Killer Spring will make a pillar of candles appear, which must be quickly compressed by alternating Right and Left clicks within 15s. Failing to do so instantly kills the player.

Mania and Twinclaw now deal 5&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts.

For Impel, it is strongly advised to use RIGHT-CLICK, or even both clicks at once, as LEFT-CLICK have a tendency to not register.

### Tier IV
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;IV [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend does not introduce new abilities, it will however use Twinclaw during Mania and will keep using Impel after the first Mania. The Twinclaw might be problematic during Mania as it can push you out of the safe zone, so make sure to not be airborn, and walk against the knockback to remain in place. Do not forget that &#160;Holy Ice is active for 1.5 seconds, allowing you to tank the Twinclaw and possibly Mania if you get pushed out. If the current Mania ring is not the one closest to the boss, Twinclaw can be avoided by moving parallel to the boss (sideway when looking at it).

Mania and Twinclaw now deal 6&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts. Impel now deals 5&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts.

The Tier&#160;IV [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend can reasonably be defeated without these upgrades however if you are careful. Obtaining them will make it more manageable if you are struggling, but will be required for Tier&#160;V.

It also feature the whole loot table of the boss, with the exception of the &#160;Enchanted Book Bundle (The One).

### Tier V
[edit | edit source]

The Tier&#160;V [A sprite icon representing Riftstalker Bloodfiend in Hypixel SkyBlock] Riftstalker Bloodfiend is the final challenge and will put your micro management skills to the test. It introduces Blood Ichor, which summons a Blood Ichor nearby with a 25 second timer. If it runs out, it instantly kills the player. It can be destroyed by letting a Twinclaw hit the Blood Ichor. Mania will now be used at 80%&#160;&#xe010;&#160;Health, 60%&#160;&#xe010;&#160;Health and 40%&#160;&#xe010;&#160;Health. The frequency of Impel is also increased.

Coagulation now deals 1.5&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts, Twinclaw deals 8&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts, Mania deals 15&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts, Impel deals 20&#160;&#xe01f;&#160;Hearts.

Mania and Impel can be survived with &#160;Holy Ice, but do not rely on it.

Be especially careful of Blood Ichor, as you only have a few Twinclaw attacks available, and make sure to pay attention to when it appears.

Make liberal use of the ability to avoid Twinclaw by moving parralel to the boss during Mania to save on &#xe003;&#160;Mana.
