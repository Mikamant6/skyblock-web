# Tutorial:Tank Guide

From Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki

Jump to navigation
Jump to search

- This tutorial is a guide.

- This tutorial has been assembled by members of the community as a helpful resource or Tutorial, instructing players on SkyBlock, and thus is not official.

- If you see missing, incorrect or out of date information, please help by contributing to the page.

The Tank class is a class within Dungeons that focuses on assisting the party by receiving incoming damage. This class depends heavily on &#xe010;&#160;Health and &#xe008;&#160;Defense, which contributes to &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health: as such, most of the gear outlined in this guide will focus on improving these stats and helping players absorb damage.

Tank is one of the cheapest classes in the early game and requires some of the least effort, although there is a significant jump in price between the mid-game and late game. In scenarios where you won't need to be near your teammates all the time and want to do some extra damage, you could potentially swap to a Berserk, Archer, or Mage set for clearing rooms and killing mobs efficiently: see Tutorial:Berserk Guide, Tutorial:Archer Guide, or Tutorial:Right-Click Mage Guide for more information.

It is best for Tanks to prioritise completing as many Dungeon Secrets as possible before your party enters the boss room, as that is the tank's responsibility. To do secrets more effectively, an Aspect of the Void (preferably with Etherwarp applied), as well as Superboom TNT and a Dungeonbreaker.

### Accessory Powers
[edit | edit source]

Magical Power is arguably the most important stat in the game, and you should aim to have 1200 Magical Power by the time you enter Master Mode - Floor VII.

While some people may think that having an Accessory Power that grants high &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health may improve your survivability, your gear should be enough to help you survive on your own. What you now need is high &#xe050;&#160;Damage in order to clear efficiently, or at least be able to kill a miniboss quickly. As a result, you should aim to use Accessory Powers with high &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage: depending on the gear you are using to clear, you could use Accessory Powers oriented towards a Berserk, Archer, or Mage.

| [Beating Heart] 

| [Beating Heart] &#160;Beating Heart

Bloody

| Bloody grants a decent amount of &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, as well as +10&#160;&#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed. It should be enough to get yourself started for clearing as a Tank.

| Early Game

| [Luxurious Spool] 

| [Luxurious Spool] &#160;Luxurious Spool

Silky

| Silky grants incredibly high &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, as well as a minuscule amount of &#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed. It is exceptional if you have at least 100%&#160;&#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance, which everyone should aim to have, although it is recommended to keep your &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage balanced.

| Early Game

| [Furball] 

| [Furball] &#160;Furball

Itchy

| Itchy balances out &#xe00d;&#160;Strength, &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, and &#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed: while it gives less &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage than Silky, it balances out all stats relatively evenly, resulting in higher damage.

| Early Game

| [Ender Monocle] 

| [Ender Monocle] &#160;Ender Monocle

Sighted

| Sighted grants a lot of &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence and minimal &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage, which will be incredibly useful if you are using a Mage setup to clear rooms efficiently. It is also the best budget power for &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence, with the only Accessory Power outclassing it being Bizarre, costing hundreds of millions of coins.

| Mid-Game

| [Magma Urchin] 

| [Magma Urchin] &#160;Magma Urchin

Hurtful

| Hurtful grants high &#xe00d;&#160;Strength, substantial &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, and decent &#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed. It is incredibly useful for clearing, but is decently rare and expensive.

| Late Game

| 

| Fortuitous

| Fortuitous grants the most &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance out of any Accessory Power, and should be what you aim to use if you are using a [Terminator] &#160;Terminator as a Tank: it is recommended to use an Archer setup in later floors of Master Mode as a Tank, since a [Terminator] &#160;Terminator heavily outdamages almost all Berserk damage.

They are buffed by Echo of Ruler, which boosts all &#xe050;&#160;Damage gained from the Ruler attributes by +20%.

Reborn

| Reborn boils down to free Dungeon Score when killing a [A sprite icon representing Prince in Hypixel SkyBlock] Prince. It is a must-have, especially for Tanks, as they prioritise doing Secrets and blowing up Crypts after their party clears the room. It is easy to obtain the shard by fusing [Flare Shard] &#160;Flare Shards with any burner shards, such as [Cod Shard] &#160;Cod Shard.

| [Mimic Shard] 

| [Mimic Shard] &#160;Mimic Shard

Faker

| Faker gives more stat bonuses from blessings, which is very helpful as it is essentially free stats for most of the clear. It is easy to obtain the shard by fusing [Prince Shard] &#160;Prince Shards with any burner shards, such as [Cod Shard] &#160;Cod Shard.

| [Inferno Demonlord Shard] 

| [Inferno Demonlord Shard] &#160;Inferno Demonlord Shard

Attack Speed

| Attack Speed grants an additional +10&#160;&#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed, which is incredibly useful as a Tank as it increases your rate of attack: additionally, it reduces the cooldown of Shortbows, which is useful when paired with a [Juju Shortbow] &#160;Juju Shortbow or [Terminator] &#160;Terminator.

| [Caiman Shard] 

| [Caiman Shard] &#160;Caiman Shard

Echo of Ruler

| Echo of Ruler is incredibly useful as it boosts the damage of Undead Ruler, Skeletal Ruler, and Humanoid Ruler from 30% to 36%, making it exceptional for clearing.

| [Kraken Shard] 

| [Kraken Shard] &#160;Kraken Shard

Vitality

| Vitality, like the name suggests, grants increased &#xe028;&#160;Vitality, which is great for increasing your incoming healing. It takes a long time to grind for, requiring several Kuudra runs, but you can buy them on the Bazaar if you're rich enough.

| [Scarf Shard] 

| [Scarf Shard] &#160;Scarf Shard

Catacombs Graduate

| Catacombs Graduate boosts ALL Class XP gain, making it a lot easier to level up your Healer class. It also is quite easy to obtain, as M2 Emerald and Obsidian chests are guaranteed to contain one, provided you reached the required class milestone to open it.
