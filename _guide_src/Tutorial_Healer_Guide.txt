# Tutorial:Healer Guide

From Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki

Jump to search

- This tutorial is a guide.

- This tutorial has been assembled by members of the community as a helpful resource or Tutorial, instructing players on SkyBlock, and thus is not official.

- If you see missing, incorrect or out of date information, please help by contributing to the page.

The Healer class is a class within Dungeons that prioritises healing your teammates and keeping them alive. Building a setup for it depends on whether you want high &#xe050;&#160;Damage and &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health: high &#xe050;&#160;Damage results in better clearing potential, whereas high &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health results in better survivability. As a result, this guide will cover both aspects of a Healer build.

You'll want to increase your &#xe014;&#160;Mending as much as possible, as &#xe014;&#160;Mending increases all outgoing healing, meaning with high &#xe014;&#160;Mending, you can increase your healing output.

It is best for Healers to focus on completing as many Dungeon Secrets as possible before your party enters the boss room.

### Enchantments
[edit | edit source]

| Enchantment
| Notes

| Armor

| Protection

| Protection and Growth are the universal enchantments that should be put on everything regardless of your situation.

| Growth

| Rejuvenate

| Rejuvenate grants a decent amount of &#xe011;&#160;Health Regen, making it great for survivability. It is relatively cheap and easy to go for, and you should aim to have Rejuvenate V on all your armour pieces as soon as possible.

| Strong Mana

| Strong Mana grants increased &#xe00d;&#160;Strength based on how much &#xe003;&#160;Mana is used near you, allowing for increased &#xe050;&#160;Damage against bosses.

| Sugar Rush

| Sugar Rush, an enchantment applicable exclusively to boots, is great for getting increased &#xe022;&#160;Speed. It can be used if you do not have a &#160;Black Cat Pet, but is also incredibly useful when paired with it as well.

| Legion

| Legion is the best Ultimate Enchantment for Healers, since you'll likely be around your teammates at all times during the boss fight.

| Weapons

| Soul Eater

| Soul Eater and Swarm are both useful Ultimate Enchantments to put on your weapons, due to their excellence in clearing. Soul Eater deals increased damage based on the last mob you hit, whereas Swarm deals more damage the more enemies are around you.

| Swarm

| Duplex

| Duplex is the best Ultimate Enchantment on Bows for raw damage and DPS, and should always be paired with a &#160;Juju Shortbow or &#160;Terminator.
