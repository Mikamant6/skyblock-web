### Fishing Stats
[edit | edit source]

There are 6 core Fishing Stats: &#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed, &#xe021;&#160;Sea Creature Chance, &#xe025;&#160;Treasure Chance, &#xe009;&#160;Double Hook Chance, &#xe02a;&#160;Trophy Chance, and ☯&#160;Fishing Wisdom. Additionally, there are 2 vital Misc Stats, and 2 vital Hunting Stat: &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find, which increases your chance for rare drops, &#xe077;&#160;Tracking, which increases the chances of catching &#xe077;&#160;Elusive Sea Creatures, &#xe05b;&#160;Hunting Fortune, which increases the chances of getting additional Shards, and ❣&#160;Charm Chance, which increases the chances of Charming mobs you kill.

### Important Formulae
[edit | edit source]

Several important formulae dictate many systems in regards to fishing. To most players, this information is irrelevant, but you may be interested in the more technical side of things.

Show/Hide Catch Time

Maximum time to catch an item:

MaxValue=400−(200×LureEnchantLevel&#x25;)

Where LureEnchantLevel represents the level of Lure you have on your fishing rod; at Lure VI, LureEnchantLevel would be 30% and be substituted in the formula for 0.3.

Number of ticks:

BaseTicks=random(200,MaxValue)

FinalTicks=BaseTicks−(FishingSpeed+BaitFishingSpeedFishingSpeedCap)×BaseTicks)

Where FinalTicks = 1 if Flash procs.

Number of seconds between catching an item and being able to reel:
Seconds=FinalTicks20

After the event of 'catching an item', there will be a random duration between 1-4s before the player is allowed to reel. This averages at 2.5s. Flash always grants the lowest duration.

Average time until you are allowed to reel:

AverageCatchTime(Seconds)=Seconds+(2.5−(2.5×QuickBiteEnchantLevel&#x25;))

Where QuickBiteEnchantLevel represents the level of Quick Bite you have on your fishing rod; at Quick Bite V, QuickBiteEnchantLevel would be 25% and be substituted in the formula for 0.25. Flash is not accounted for in this formula.

Show/Hide Loot Drop Chances

There are different methods to calculate the drop chances of an item, depending on its type and its base drop chance.

LootChance=BaseChance×(1+LootingEnchantLevel&#x25;+LuckEnchantLevel&#x25;)

Where LootingEnchantLevel represents the level of Looting you have on your weapon; at Looting V, LootingEnchantLevel would be 75% and be substituted in the formula for 0.75.
Similarly, LuckEnchantLevel represents the level of Luck you have on your weapon; at Luck VII, LuckEnchantLevel would be 35% and be substituted in the formula for 0.35.
If the desired item is not a piece of Armor, LuckEnchantLevel&#x25; is set to 0.

If the desired item has a drop chance of ≤5%:
Loot=Amount×(LootChance×(1+MagicFind+PetLuck100))

If the desired item is not a Pet, PetLuck is set to 0.
If the desired item has a drop chance of ≥5%, it will not be affected by &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find. Modifiers, such as Looting, may push the drop chances of items above 5% and cause it to not be affected by &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find.

If the desired item has a drop chance of ≥5%:

Loot=Amount×LootChance

Show/Hide Sea Creature Weights

Each Sea Creature has its own Weight. A sea creature's Weight can be viewed in the Sea Creature Guide, accessible through /scg, or on their respective article.

WeightTotalWeight=Chance
ChancePerCatch=Chance×SeaCreatureChance+DoubleHookChance100

### Hotspots
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Fishing Hotspots

Hotspots are temporary rings that appear in water and lava on certain islands. After speaking to [A sprite icon representing Hattie in Hypixel SkyBlock] Hattie in the &#xe067; Backwater Bayou, you gain the ability to fish in Hotspots.

Fishing in a Hotspot grants the player one of the following stats:

- +15&#160;&#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed

- +5&#160;&#xe021;&#160;Sea Creature Chance

- +2&#160;&#xe009;&#160;Double Hook Chance

- +5&#160;&#xe02a;&#160;Trophy Chance

- Exclusive to the &#xe067; Lotus Atoll and &#xe067; Crimson Isle

- +1&#160;&#xe025;&#160;Treasure Chance

Stats granted from Fishing Hotspots are doubled if a &#160;Hotspot Sinker is applied to the player's Fishing Rod.

The Hotspot in the &#xe067; Hotspot Haven grants the Chance to catch Giant Water Bug Shard effect, replacing all GREAT CATCHES and OUTSTANDING CATCHES with varying quantities of &#160;Giant Water Bug Shards: this is affected by &#xe05b;&#160;Hunting Fortune.

### Weather
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Weather

Weather is a system in SkyBlock that occurs in specific intervals, granting island-wide effects. Some types of Weather grant fishing-oriented benefits, even outside Fishing islands.

Mild Weather conditions start every 60 minutes and last for 20 minutes. Every 3rd Mild Weather condition will instead be EXTREME.

All Weather conditions grant stat boosts, and EXTREME weather conditions have an additional bonus effect. Mild Weather grants effects in the &#xe067; Spider's Den and &#xe067; Lotus Atoll as well.

[A sprite icon representing Professor Wynd in Hypixel SkyBlock] Professor Wynd, who is found in the &#xe067; Weather Station, can also alert the player of up to 4 types of upcoming weather when added as an Abiphone Contact. &#160;1&#160;Weather Wand is required to unlock [A sprite icon representing Professor Wynd in Hypixel SkyBlock] Professor Wynd's contact.

## Leveling Up Fishing
[edit | edit source]

&#160;Fishing is notoriously one of the most tedious skills to level up, mainly early on prior to &#160;Fishing&#160;XXVII (27) at which you unlock the &#160;Magma Rod and some of your first Lava Sea Creatures.

### Fishing 0-19
[edit | edit source]

The best methods for leveling up &#160;Fishing prior to &#160;Fishing&#160;XVII (17) and unlocking the &#160;Starter Lava Rod, or &#160;Fishing&#160;XIX (19) and the &#160;Topaz Rod, are:

- The Fish Family: Filling out The Fish Family at [A sprite icon representing Coral in Hypixel SkyBlock] Coral. If you have the budget, only 20 'the Fish' items are required to reach &#160;Fishing&#160;XIX (19), and 27 for &#160;Fishing&#160;XX (20).

- Atoll Fishing: Sea Creatures from the &#xe067; Lotus Atoll yield higher amounts of &#160;Fishing&#160;XP. While they have higher &#xe010;&#160;Health and deal more &#xe050;&#160;Damage, it is not very noticeable if you have good survivability.

- Bayou Fishing: The &#xe067; Backwater Bayou is where you will primarily be fishing for the most part in the early game, and its Sea Creatures grant both useful items and decent XP: as a result, this will be your main active source of &#160;Fishing&#160;XP in the early game, with other sources being mostly passive.

- &#160;Fishing Minions and &#160;Lily Pad Minions: Both minions grant easy passive XP: it is recommended to place a &#160;Super Compactor 3000, &#160;Enchanted Lava Bucket, and &#160;Minion Expander, alongside &#160;Large Storage for more storage.

- &#160;Lily Pad Minions are better for &#160;Fishing&#160;XP, while &#160;Fishing Minions are better for collecting mass quantities of fishing drops. &#160;Clay Minions are the worst out of the three.

- Chumming: If you are with another player, you can deposit &#160;Chum into their &#160;Empty Chum Bucket for quick XP: this requires a &#160;Luminous Bracelet, which grants +10&#160;&#160;Fishing&#160;XP when depositing &#160;Chum in someone else's bucket.

- Using /gfs Chum 64 is an easy way to semi-automate this process: mods exist that allow you to bind this command to a keypress, but by pressing the ↑ key after opening the chat, you can quickly issue the same command again, provided you have not sent any other messages or ran any other commands.

- Opening Cans of Worms: Opening &#160;Can of Worms allows you to convert coins to XP, rewarding +500&#160;&#160;Fishing&#160;XP every 3.5 cans, +1,000&#160;&#160;Fishing&#160;XP every 7 cans, and +2,000&#160;&#160;Fishing&#160;XP every 14 cans. While expensive and slow, this method is good if you are in the very early stages and want to unlock [A sprite icon representing Stridersurfer in Hypixel SkyBlock] Stridersurfers as soon as possible.

### Fishing 19-27
[edit | edit source]

The stretch between &#160;Fishing&#160;XX (20), when you unlock the &#160;Rod of Legends and a multitude of Rod Parts, and &#160;Fishing&#160;XXVII (27) is notorious for being slow and tedious. However, there is still an efficient way to reach high levels quickly:

- Stridersurfer Fishing: Lava fishing in the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh next to [A sprite icon representing Sawyer in Hypixel SkyBlock] Sawyer is popular as the [A sprite icon representing Stridersurfer in Hypixel SkyBlock] Stridersurfers you spawn grant high amounts of XP. This spot is often crowded and contested, though you may be able to fish here in a group.

- Note that it is perfectly viable to fish Stridersurfers with a &#160;Starter Lava Rod if you do not have a &#160;Topaz Rod or &#160;Magma Rod.

Additionally, there are three other methods of unlocking &#160;Fishing&#160;XXVII (27), though they may yield less &#160;Fishing&#160;XP:

- Magma Core Fishing: At Heart of the Mountain&#160;VI (6), when you have unlocked both the &#160;Topaz Rod and the Subterranean Fisher perk (requiring 945,621&#32;᠅&#8201;Gemstone Powder to max) fishing in the &#xe067; Magma Fields for &#160;Magma Cores is superior.

- Bayou Fishing: Fishing in the water pool around &#160;48,&#8239;75,&#8239;28 to &#160;55,&#8239;75,&#8239;20 in the &#xe067; Backwater Bayou is the second-best method for XP at this stage: due to the way it is structured, mobs will automatically fall off the edge and accumulate underneath you, where someone else will likely kill the mobs for you.

- Bayou Hotspot Fishing: Fishing in &#xe067; Backwater Bayou hotspots is generally the third-best method for XP at this stage.

### Fishing 27-50
[edit | edit source]

Once you have achieved &#160;Fishing&#160;XXVII (27), levelling up &#160;Fishing will be vastly faster. However, killing [A sprite icon representing Stridersurfer in Hypixel SkyBlock] Stridersurfers remains the best option if you cannot kill Sea Creatures on the &#xe067; Crimson Isle quickly and efficiently.

- Crimson Isle Fishing: Fishing in the &#xe067; Crimson Isle is the best way to gain &#160;Fishing&#160;XP at this stage.

- A &#160;Hyperion with &#160;Wither Impact is the best and most efficient weapon. Once you reach &#160;Fishing&#160;XLVII (47), a &#160;Flaming Flay is better for a select few mobs, such as [A sprite icon representing Wiki Tiki in Hypixel SkyBlock] Wiki Tikis, [A sprite icon representing Titanoboa in Hypixel SkyBlock] Titanoboas, or [A sprite icon representing Ragnarok in Hypixel SkyBlock] Ragnaroks. A &#160;Midas Staff is a useful alternative to a fully-scrolled &#160;Hyperion, though it lacks the healing capabilities of the latter.

- A &#160;Soul Whip, or any sword with high damage and clearing capabilities, such as a &#160;Giant's Sword or &#160;Dark Claymore, may suffice, though it is less efficient.

- Due to specific mobs having high resistance to magic damage - such as the &#160;Hyperion or &#160;Midas Staff - a &#160;Soul Whip or &#160;Flaming Flay is recommended for mobs such as [A sprite icon representing Ragnarok in Hypixel SkyBlock] Ragnarok, [A sprite icon representing Wiki Tiki in Hypixel SkyBlock] Wiki Tikis, [A sprite icon representing Titanoboa in Hypixel SkyBlock] Titanoboas, and [A sprite icon representing Nutcracker in Hypixel SkyBlock] Nutcrackers.

- Without a &#160;Hyperion or &#160;Flaming Flay, lava fishing is marginally worse than [A sprite icon representing Stridersurfer in Hypixel SkyBlock] Stridersurfer fishing.

In order to be able to catch [A sprite icon representing Thunder in Hypixel SkyBlock] Thunder and [A sprite icon representing Lord Jawbus in Hypixel SkyBlock] Lord Jawbus, you must have unlocked Novice Trophy Hunter and Adept Trophy Hunter respectively.

## Maximising Stats

### Maximising Magic Find
[edit | edit source]

&#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find increases the drop chances and quantities of items from mobs: as a result, to maximise your profits, high &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find is naturally imperative.

While &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find has historically been tied to quick, last-minute swapping, all sources of unconditional &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find act as a "set-and-forget" system where you will have its effects active at all times.

Sources of &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find are either unconditional or conditional.

- Most unconditional, or global, sources appear in the /stats menu;

- All unconditional sources do not appear in this menu.

Multipliers on &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find (&#xe01a;&#160;MF) only apply to sources that appear in the /stats menu. All other sources remain unaffected and will instead be added as a flat amount.

[edit | edit source]

While it is best to complete the &#xe067; Lotus Atoll after completion of Trophy Fish, due to collecting Trophy Frogs granting little in regards to progression, this guide assumes you are completing Trophy Frogs without having accessed the &#xe067; Crimson Isle.

## Trophy Fishing Guide
[edit | edit source]

Main pages: Odger and Trophy Fish

### Tiers of Trophy Fish
[edit | edit source]

Trophy Fish are special items that can be caught on the &#xe067; Crimson Isle while lava fishing. A total of 18 unique Trophy Fish exist, each coming in 4 tiers: Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond. In total, there are 72 Trophy Fish.

It is recommended to start Trophy Fishing at &#160;Fishing&#160;XXXVI (36) to unlock &#160;Bronze Hunter Armor, and eventually &#160;Silver Hunter Armor at &#160;Fishing&#160;XLV (45). Alternatively, you can begin Trophy Fishing at &#160;Fishing&#160;X (10), but preferably between &#160;Fishing&#160;XXIV (24) and &#160;Fishing&#160;XXVII (27): this is to prevent Sea Creatures from being fished up.

The game rolls for the rarest Trophy Fish first, proceeding down each tier. As a result, if any Trophy Fish is rarer than the specific Trophy Fish you are targeting, it may adversely affect your rates.

A Pity system exists for Trophy Fish. After 100 or 600 catches of any specific Trophy Fish, you are guaranteed to catch a Gold or Diamond Trophy Fish.

### Trophy Fishing Tips
[edit | edit source]

Here are some additional tips that will make Trophy fishing slightly easier.

- If you intend to use a clean &#160;Starter Lava Rod to roll for &#160;Vanille, you can also target &#160;Slugfish at the same time.

- Holding a Fishing Rod with high &#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed - like a &#160;Hellfire Rod or a &#160;Rod of the Sea, the latter of which does not work in lava but has higher &#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed - and quickly swapping to a &#160;Starter Lava Rod and casting before your &#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed is updated means you can get 350&#160;&#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed for a short duration.

- Having between ~110-118&#160;&#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed is optimal for targeting &#160;Slugfish, as it typically gives you catches at around 10s with a &#160;Slug Pet. You may wish to intentionally lower your &#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed to achieve this. Ways to decrease your &#xe00c;&#160;Fishing Speed include:

- Disabling the &#160;Glowing Mush Mixin

- Equipping a &#160;Burnt Texts

- Using &#160;Corrupted Bait

- Do not stack &#160;Slugfish with &#160;Volcanic Stonefish: instead, it is best to stack &#160;Slugfish with an EPIC Trophy Fish - preferably &#160;Soul Fish as it is the most isolated one.

- You can target &#160;Golden Fish while fishing normally, as it is independent of other Trophy Fish and works on a timer system.

- Attempt &#160;Obfuscated-1, &#160;Obfuscated-2, and &#160;Obfuscated-3 last.

- Some notes about Dyes, which are extremely rare, cosmetic items that can be applied to your Armor.

- You can drop Dyes from cocooned mobs.

- You can drop Dyes if your targeted mob is killed by &#xe00b;&#160;Ferocity, Venomous, or Fire Aspect.

- Both [A sprite icon representing Bayou Sludge in Hypixel SkyBlock] Bayou Sludges and [A sprite icon representing Bayou Sludgling in Hypixel SkyBlock] Bayou Sludglings can drop Dyes.

- It is recommended to set your &#160;Beacon to &#xe077;&#160;Tracking rather than &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find, though both are equally useful.

However, there is one last tip that this guide has to share with you:

- Not following this guide.
"What?!" you might be thinking. "Not following this guide? So what was the point of writing all this?"
And the point of it was to provide some structure and recommend some tips and progression if you wish to get into &#160;Fishing, are struggling with it, or want to know where to go next. But this guide details with some very complicated tech and some very intricate strategies, and &#160;Fishing by itself is a skill with lots of intricate and niche tech tied into it!
Not everyone has to be hyper-optimised. I certainly am not hyper-optimised in the slightest. While not necessarily the most efficient, the fun in SkyBlock is playing at your own pace. Don't feel that you are constrained by what this guide tells you to do. Certainly hold it into consideration, though, but don't hold yourself strictly to its standard.
Yes, the end goal should be hyper-optimisation and being hypermaxed. But don't stress out about it, and don't rush it. Appreciate the depth of the ocean, but enjoy the swim.
Thanks for reading this guide!
- Whami <3
