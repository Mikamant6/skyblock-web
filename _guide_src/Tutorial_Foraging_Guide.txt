## Foraging Stats
[edit | edit source]

Unlike other skills such as &#160;Combat, there are only 4&#160;Foraging Stats: &#xe023;&#160;Sweep, &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune, ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom and &#xe02e;&#160;Timber.

| Stat

| Description

| Formulas

| &#xe023;&#160;Sweep

| &#xe023;&#160;Sweep increases how many blocks are broken with each chop. For example, 10&#160;&#xe023;&#160;Sweep would break 10 logs at a time. However, some trees have higher ⧉&#160;Toughness, which reduces the effect of &#xe023;&#160;Sweep.
The scaling is logarithmic, meaning the more &#xe023;&#160;Sweep you have, the less effect getting more &#xe023;&#160;Sweep has. Throwing an Axe gives a 50% &#xe023;&#160;Sweep penalty.

&#xe023;&#160;Sweep also affects how fast you can break a log, making it very useful.

| There is no consistent formula for blocks broken. See the required values here.
Ticks to break=⌊20011×ToughnessSweep⌋

| &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune

| &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune increases the chance to get multiple logs per drop. When paired with high &#xe023;&#160;Sweep, this can lead to a high quantity of logs being obtained at a time.

| Guaranteed Extra Logs=⌊Foraging Fortune100⌋
Chance For 1 Additional Log=(Foraging Fortune)mod100

| ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom

| ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom increases the amount of &#160;Foraging&#160;XP gained. If you want to get to high &#160;Foraging levels, it is best to get a high amount of ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom.

| ForagingXPGained=BaseForagingXP×(1+ForagingWisdom100)

| &#xe02e;&#160;Timber

| &#xe02e;&#160;Timber grants a chance for the entire tree to be felled on the first melee axe cut. It only rolls once per tree and has 3 sources currently in the game. It is important to increase this stat as it decreases the average time spent at each tree.

## General Progression
[edit | edit source]

This section goes over each part of progression generally; the rest of the guide should also be read to get an idea of what upgrades to get specifically.

To start the &#160;Foraging Skill, talk to [A sprite icon representing Lumber Jack in Hypixel SkyBlock] Lumber Jack and complete his quest. You should also buy a &#160;Beginner Foraging Sack from the [A sprite icon representing Lumber Merchant in Hypixel SkyBlock] Lumber Merchant for 250 Coins.

### The Park
[edit | edit source]

&#xe067; The Park is an area split up into five sections, each for a different type of wood. Simply progress through the quests on each island to obtain a full set of &#160;Park Armor. You should also craft yourself a &#160;Spruce Axe with [Spruce Log] &#160;96x&#160;Spruce Logs. Continue breaking Jungle Trees until you are &#160;Foraging&#160;XII (12). Craft an &#160;Ocelot Pet as it is useful for ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom. You could also consider upgrading to a &#160;Small Foraging Sack.

### Moonglade Marsh
[edit | edit source]

If on a Classic profile, buy a &#160;Fig Hew immediately after unlocking the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh, and &#160;Fig Armor immediately after getting &#160;Foraging&#160;XV (15). If on an ♲&#160;Ironman profile, use a &#160;Seriously Damaged Axe instead, until you have enough materials to craft a &#160;Fig Hew, and buy &#160;Canopy Armor. Do only Fig Trees at this point. Focus on upgrading to a &#160;Figstone Splitter. If you do not have enough materials for it after getting &#160;Foraging&#160;XV (15), buying them from the Bazaar is a good option. Start getting some of the cheaper upgrades, such as Shards, Reforges and Enchantments. Focus on Starlyn Contests and HotF progression.

### Torrhus Canyon
[edit | edit source]

The next &#160;Foraging island, the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon is unlocked at HotF Tier 4. However, it should not be moved to immediately, due to the high ⧉&#160;Toughness making the Helix Trees hard to cut quickly. Some of the Attribute Shards there can be very useful back on the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh, so they should be obtained immediately after unlocking the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon. The important ones that are easy to obtain are &#160;Solar Shards, &#160;Ember Shards and &#160;Pangolin Shards. You can also follow others who are cutting down trees and hunt the mobs that spawn with a &#160;Lasso to get &#160;Groundhog Shards, &#160;Puck Shards, &#160;Drybark Shards and &#160;Grizzly Bear Shards. Once you reach 600&#160;&#xe023;&#160;Sweep (or less on ♲&#160;Ironman), you can move to the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon as your main island. Get &#160;Helix Chopper and &#160;Helix Armor as soon as possible. Follow similar strategies as on the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh of progressing through [A sprite icon representing Miria in Hypixel SkyBlock] Miria's shop and getting the best value upgrades you haven't gotten already.

### Leveling
[edit | edit source]

&#160;Foraging is a fairly quick skill to level up, making it good for beginners. To level up as fast as possible, maximising your ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom is key. The easiest way to do this initially is to use an &#160;Ocelot Pet. Apart from that, nothing special should be done to get &#160;Foraging&#160;XP faster. Once you reach &#160;Foraging&#160;L (50), the level cap needs to be increased to level up further. +1 level cap can be obtained from each of the &#160;Fig Log, &#160;Mangrove Log and &#160;Helix Log collections at Level IX, +2 can be obtained from [A sprite icon representing Agatha in Hypixel SkyBlock] Agatha's shop, and +2 can be obtained from [A sprite icon representing Miria in Hypixel SkyBlock] Miria's shop. It costs &#160;750&#160;Agatha's Coupon, &#160;7&#160;Agatha's Prize, &#160;750&#160;Miria's Coupon and &#160;7&#160;Miria's Prize in total.

## The Park
[edit | edit source]

Main page: The Park

&#xe067; The Park is an area split up into five sections, each for a different type of wood. A short quest needs to be completed to get to the next section, and either a piece of &#160;Park Armor or a &#160;Campfire Initiate Badge is given.

- The &#xe067; Birch Park contains &#160;Birch, whose collection most notably has the &#160;Foraging Sack. It also has a pond for fishing, and you can give coins to [A sprite icon representing Vanessa in Hypixel SkyBlock] Vanessa to make it rain and spawn [A sprite icon representing Squid in Hypixel SkyBlock] Squids.

- The &#xe067; Spruce Woods contains &#160;Spruce, whose collection most notably has the &#160;Spruce Axe and &#160;Wolf Pet. You can start the Woods Race here, and talk to the [A sprite icon representing Melancholic Viking in Hypixel SkyBlock] Melancholic Viking and complete his quest for some items that may have niche uses.

- The &#xe067; Dark Thicket contains &#160;Dark Oak, whose collection most notably has the &#160;Armor of Growth that upgrades into &#160;Mastiff Armor. The Trials of Fire can be found here, as well as the &#xe067; Howling Cave, a zone for Wolf Slayer.

- The &#xe067; Savanna Woodland contains &#160;Acacia, whose collection has the &#160;Repelling Candle and &#160;Acacia Birdhouse. [A sprite icon representing Master Tactician Funk in Hypixel SkyBlock] Master Tactician Funk is found here, who sells items. The &#xe067; Soul Cave, another zone for Wolf Slayer can also be found here. [A sprite icon representing Melody in Hypixel SkyBlock] Melody, along with the Harp is also present here.

- The &#xe067; Jungle Island contains &#160;Jungle Wood, whose collection has the &#160;Treecapitator and the &#160;Ocelot Pet. The Romero and Juliette quest can be found and started here, as well as the &#xe067; Spirit Cave, another zone for Wolf Slayer. This island is also where [A sprite icon representing Juliette in Hypixel SkyBlock] Juliette resides, the starting point of the &#160;Ring of Love quest.

## Galatea
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Galatea

Upon entering the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh for the first time, you'll be introduced to Starlyn Contests, Tree Gifts, the Heart of the Forest, and &#160;Hunting.

- Starlyn Contests are contests held by the Starlyn Sisters where you accumulate points by doing activities on &#xe067; Galatea, similar to Jacob's Farming Contests.

- Tree Gifts are rewards given to the player after finishing chopping down trees in &#xe067; Galatea. They have to cut at least 10% of the tree to get any rewards, 20% to get 50% more rewards, or 33% to get double rewards. Rewards include Heart of the Forest XP, &#160;Foraging XP, Forest Whispers or Desert Whispers, and rare drops.

- &#160;Hunting is a skill where players hunt creatures through a multitude of different methods for Attribute Shards, which grant buffs. This tutorial will not cover &#160;Hunting, but it will give information on what Attribute Shards are good for &#160;Foraging.

[edit | edit source]

Main page: Starlyn Contests

They last for 1 day in SkyBlock Time, which translates to 20 minutes in real life, and there is no break in between contests.

### Strategies
[edit | edit source]

- &#160;Lushlilac bushes gives 9 points each harvested, which can easily allow for anyone with an &#160;Aspect of the End to reach LEGENDARY in the contest. Additionally, the Berry Enjoyer attribute from the &#160;Bullfrog Shard has a chance to double points acquired from this method, which can allow even MYTHIC scores to be obtained consistently. However, this makes next to zero coins, and thus is not recommended past the early game.

- At low &#xe023;&#160;Sweep values, only Fig Trees are worth going for. Despite granting more points, Mangrove Trees shouldn't be gone for until having at least 300&#160;&#xe023;&#160;Sweep, due to having much higher ⧉&#160;Toughness.

- On the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon, chopping Helix Trees is by far the best strategy. The amount of points Tree Gifts give is basically negligible, so larger trees are more efficient for contests specifically.

- Floor Drops can be found as particles in the ground, which you should never ignore as they can give &#160;Mudworm Shards or high amounts of &#160;Fig Logs, &#160;Mangrove Logs or &#160;Helix Logs, as well as Desert Whispers on the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon.

- &#160;Fishing, especially when combined with catching most sea creatures via a &#160;Pocket Black Hole, can provide high amounts of points, but is not a consistently viable strategy to use for LEGENDARY results unless you have a good setup.

- Killing [A sprite icon representing Chillblade in Hypixel SkyBlock] Chillblades and [A sprite icon representing Chillshot in Hypixel SkyBlock] Chillshots can give a high amount of points, and are needed for other items.

### Agatha's and Miria's Shops
[edit | edit source]

[A sprite icon representing Agatha in Hypixel SkyBlock] Agatha's and [A sprite icon representing Miria in Hypixel SkyBlock] Miria's Shops can give valuable items and boosts that help with progression. Most things are bought with &#160;Agatha's or &#160;Miria's Coupons.

Other than items, the most important things from the shop are Agatha's/Miria's Power, Fig/Mangrove/Helix Personal Best and Fig/Mangrove/Helix Fortune. Agatha's and Miria's Power grants +25&#160;&#xe023;&#160;Sweep each, a significant boost, and cost &#160;200&#160;Agatha's/&#160;Miria's Coupons to max. The Fig/Mangrove/Helix Personal Best perks require increasing the collection of each log during a contest, with a max of 100,000 collection, giving +10%&#160;&#xe023;&#160;Sweep each, for a total of +30% global &#xe023;&#160;Sweep. The Fig/Mangrove/Helix Fortune perks give &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune for each type of wood, as the names suggest. They give up to 200&#160;&#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune for the wood types each, but the later tiers cost a lot of &#160;Coupons and materials.

### Throwing Axes
[edit | edit source]

Axes can be thrown to chop logs from afar. They have a cooldown and the amount of logs that they cut is reduced by 50%. This makes throwing your axe repeatedly inefficient. There are upgrades that reduce the cooldown and the penalty, but they should still only be thrown occasionally.

### Styles
[edit | edit source]

Different types of trees have different ways that they need to be cut to be more efficient. If a tree is cut in the wrong way, the amount of logs you cut will be reduced by 50%.

Fig Trees should be cut with the trunk first, then the branches.

Mangrove Trees should be cut with the branches first, then the trunk, then the roots.

Helix Trees should be cut with the beige logs first, then the red logs.

It does not matter how every other type of tree is cut.

### Starborn Temples
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Starborn Temple

The Starborn Temples are places on the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh and the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon that take donations to grant useful temporary buffs and make you a Benefactor. You must first complete a puzzle near or inside the temple to unlock it. Unlocking a Temple for the first time also gives you a permanent stat boost that is dependent on how many people are currently a Benefactor. The &#xe067; Forest Temple on the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh grants &#160;Foraging buffs, whereas the &#xe067; Desert Temple on the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon grants &#160;Hunting buffs. A player cannot be a Benefactor to both Temples at the same time.

### Beacons
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Moonglade Beacon

There are 2 Beacons, one on the &#xe067; Torrhus Canyon and one on the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh. Players can tune the Beacons through a minigame, giving it 1 Stack each, lasting 20 minutes. Each Stack gives a base of +0.5&#160;&#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune for the types of wood on the island to all players on the island, up to a max of 20 Stacks. &#160;Beaconmite Shards can rarely be found when tuning a Beacon, which make Beacons stronger and give a chance to drop &#160;Signal Enhancers from Tree Gifts. The Beacons can be upgraded using &#160;Signal Enhancers by doing a more difficult minigame, costing &#160;10&#160;Signal Enhancers each to max. This grants +10&#160;&#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune per Stack.

### Forest Essence Shop
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Forest Essence

The Forest Essence Shop, accessed through [A sprite icon representing Rosemary in Hypixel SkyBlock] Rosemary on the &#xe067; Moonglade Marsh can provide useful buffs such as more &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune or ☯&#160;Foraging Wisdom. However, it is not as important as HotF or Attribute Shards. It costs [Forest Essence] &#160;34,875x&#160;Forest Essence to max every &#160;Foraging perk. Try to balance the Axed, Lumberjack and Forest Training Perks by spending a similar amount of Essence on each of them.

### Heart of the Forest
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Heart of the Forest

Heart of the Forest is an upgrade tree similar to Heart of the Mountain that provides very useful buffs. Nodes are unlocked with Tokens of the Forest, and they are upgraded with Forest Whispers or Desert Whispers. You can gain Heart of the Forest XP to progress through the tree, up to Tier 8. Each tier gives Tokens of the Forest to spend and SkyBlock XP. Some nodes are not able to be upgraded past unlocking them. Nodes in Tier 3 and below use Forest Whispers, while nodes in Tier 4 and above use Desert Whispers.

#### Perks
[edit | edit source]

##### Tier 1
[edit | edit source]

- Sweep: A very useful perk, granting up to +50&#160;&#xe023;&#160;Sweep. It should be one of the first to be maxed.

##### Tier 2
[edit | edit source]

- Damage Boost: Not a very useful perk, as damage from axes is generally not a problem.

- Luck of the Forest: A pretty useful perk that can provide a small buff to your Tree Gift loot.

- Foraging Fortune: Another very useful perk, and should be leveled only slightly slower than Sweep. It can grant up to 150&#160;&#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune.

- Collector: Not very useful, as farming &#160;Lushlilacs or &#160;Sea Lumies is not profitable.

- Axe Toss: Not very useful, as throwing of axes is slow and inefficient.

##### Tier 3
[edit | edit source]

- Deep Waters: Useful if you are trying to farm Water Shards or &#160;Sea Lumies, but not useful for Foraging itself.

- Hunter's Luck: Only useful for &#160;Hunting.

- Galatea's Might: Usually not very useful, but can help with the Forest Strength perk.

##### Tier 4
[edit | edit source]

- Lottery: Gives decent buffs to either &#xe054;&#160;Helix Fortune, &#xe054;&#160;Mangrove Fortune, &#xe054;&#160;Fig Fortune or &#xe023;&#160;Sweep.

- Foraging Madness: A decent buff that should be gotten with spare Tokens.

- Iron Lungs: Useful if you are trying to farm Water Shards, but not useful for Foraging itself.

- 250 Gifts: Can also be useful for getting more loot from Tree Gifts.

- Daily Wishes: Can be useful for getting large amounts of Forest Whispers, and is very cheap to max.

- Early Bird: Gives a significant buff for the first 250 Trees cut per day.

- Precision Cutting: Generally not useful as each log takes a small amount of time to cut, and it is only a small buff.

##### Tier 5
[edit | edit source]

- Tree Whisperer: Somewhat useful for extra Forest Whispers.

- Center of the Forest: Maxing this perk is very useful because of the Tokens of the Forest and extra &#xe023;&#160;Sweep it gives. You are not able to refund the Forest Whispers you spend on this perk, unlike the other perks.

- Free Trial: Isn't very useful after you are able to get LEGENDARY, as it only gives a &#160;Basic Safari Ticket.

##### Tier 6
[edit | edit source]

- Homing Axe: Often reported to be bugged and doesn't do anything. Only get it to get to another perk.

- Forest Fisher: Only useful for &#160;Fishing.

- Strength Boost: Mostly only useful when combined with the Forest Strength perk.

- Starlyn Supreme: Very useful for Starlyn Contests, making it easier to get high brackets.

- Speed Boost: Can be useful if you are not already at the cap of 400&#160;&#xe022;&#160;Speed, but other than that it is only useful when combined with the Forest Speed perk.

- Efficient Forager: Quite useful for leveling the &#160;Foraging skill.

- Maniac Slicer: Can be useful for a burst of &#xe023;&#160;Sweep for a short time, but it can be hard to get a large amount of &#xe003;&#160;Mana in a &#160;Foraging setup. Using the Bizarre or Sighted powers can help with this.

##### Tier 7
[edit | edit source]

- Half Empty: Can be quite useful for extra &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune and &#xe023;&#160;Sweep, but it is dependent on the other players on the island.

- Ricochet: Slightly useful to get a head start on trees around you.

- Half Full: Can be quite useful for extra &#xe054;&#160;Foraging Fortune and &#xe023;&#160;Sweep, but it is dependent on the other players on the island.

##### Tier 8
[edit | edit source]

- Monster Hunter: Not useful, since hunting monsters don't give very much whispers and aren't used in the first place.

- Forest Speed: Requires maximizing your &#xe022;&#160;Speed stat. Using the Buttery Power and a &#160;God Potion can help with this.

- Essence Fortune: This perk can be useful if you have not maxed the Forest Essence shop yet.

- Timber: This perk is extremely useful, and significantly decreases the average time spent on each tree.

- Two-for-one: This perk is decent for getting more Critter Safari Tickets, but if you do more &#160;Foraging than the Critter Safari, it doesn't do much.

- Forest Strength: Similar to Forest Speed, it requires maximizing you &#xe00d;&#160;Strength. Using the Forceful, Shaded or Strong Accessory Powers can help with this.

- Beekeeper: Slightly useful for &#160;Hunting using Honeycomb and Honeyhives, but not &#160;Foraging.

#### Optimal Tree
[edit | edit source]

Tier 1: Unlock Sweep.

Tier 2: Unlock Foraging Fortune and Luck of the Forest.

Tier 3: Unlock Collector and Axe Toss.

Tier 4: Reset the Tree and unlock Sweep, Foraging Fortune, Luck of the Forest, Hunter's Luck, 250 Gifts, Iron Lungs, and Foraging Madness.

Tier 5: Unlock Lottery and Daily Wishes. Get Tree Whisperer with one of the extra Tokens of the Mountain you get from Centre of the Forest, and Early Bird with the other.

Tier 6: Unlock Starlyn Supreme and Speed Boost.

Tier 7: Unlock Efficient Forager and Half Full.

Tier 8: Reset the Tree and unlock Sweep, Foraging Fortune, Hunter's Luck, 250 Gifts, Daily Wishes, Iron Lungs, Foraging Madness, Lottery, Starlyn Supreme, Speed Boost, Efficient Forager, Richochet, Half Full, Timber, Forest Strength, Essence Fortune, and Forest Speed.
