- If you see missing, incorrect or out of date information, please help by contributing to the page.

The Right-Click Mage is a subsect of the Mage class within Dungeons that focuses heavily on Abilities, &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence, and &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage. They often depend on using their abilities to attack, hence the name Right-Click, as opposed to Left-Click Mages who use the Mage Staff ability to attack. This guide will refer to them purely as Mages for the sake of brevity.

Unlike Berserks and Archers, Mages do not rely on &#xe00d;&#160;Strength or &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage to increase their damage output: instead, they increase their &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence. This is important because &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage scales with &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence.

Mages often lack &#xe008;&#160;Defense and &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health, reducing their survivability: as a result, this guide will also suggest gear that grants high &#xe010;&#160;Effective Health and survivability while sacrificing &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence, which can be made up for using the Necrotic reforge in most cases.

Before you read this guide, it is important to note Magical Power. Arguably the most important and often overlooked stat in the game, you should have around 300 between Floors 1-3, 550 by Floors 5 and 6, 700 by Floor 7, 950 by Master Mode F3, 1200 by Master Mode F5, and at least 1500 by Master Mode F7. You increase your Magical Power by having Accessories in your Accessory Bag, and harness it by using Accessory Powers. 

Because Mage can be expensive or difficult to use early-game, it is advised to use the Berserk class until you have completion of The Catacombs - Floor IV. It is also highly advised to not rush entering the Catacombs.

### Enchantments
[edit | edit source]

Given that high &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence and &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage is crucial for Mages, it is important to select Enchantments that both work with ability damage instead of melee damage and increase both stats. It is best to put as many enchantments are you can on your weapons, and there are some you should use over others.

| Notes

| Smite is very effective against &#xe084;&#160;Undead mobs, which make up the bulk of the Catacombs.

| While Drain is far superior for melee damage, it does not work on magic damage, meaning you should use Life Steal instead.

| Mana Vampire

| These 4 mutually exclusive enchants will be very useful as you will be using &#xe003;&#160;Mana very often as a Mage, meaning that you will be able to receive a lot of healing, &#xe008;&#160;Defense, &#xe00d;&#160;Strength, or &#xe00b;&#160;Ferocity.
