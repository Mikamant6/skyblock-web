# Pet Items

From Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki

Jump to navigation
Jump to search
See also: Pet Upgrades, Pet Skin, and Pet Food

Pet Items are items that can be given to Pets to improve them. Each pet can only hold one item at a time. To use them, equip a pet and right-click the pet while holding a pet item.

Pet items are consumed on use. Upon equipping a new item, the old item can be swapped out for the new item for a fee. An existing item can be removed with the &#160;Super Scrubber, bought from [A sprite icon representing Plumber Joe in Hypixel SkyBlock] Plumber Joe.

## Retrieving Pet Items
[edit | edit source]

Pet Items can be retrieved from Pets by applying a new Pet Item onto a Pet. Doing so refunds the previous Pet Item and costs Coins to apply and is as follows:

| Rarity

| Cost

| COMMON

| 25,000 Coins

| UNCOMMON

| 50,000 Coins

| RARE

| 100,000 Coins

| EPIC

| 250,000 Coins

| LEGENDARY

| 500,000 Coins

| MYTHIC

| 1,000,000 Coins

| DIVINE

| 2,500,000 Coins

A &#160;Super Scrubber can be used to remove a Pet Item without applying a new one: the cost still applies and the Super Scrubber is consumed in the process.

## Trivia
[edit | edit source]

- All Pet XP boosts work multiplicatively together, such as &#160;Taming, [A sprite icon representing Diana in Hypixel SkyBlock] Diana, and the &#160;Beastmaster Crest.

- If player has multiple of the same Pet Item in their hotbar and they right-click their pet, it will always consume the item in the slot closest to the first, regardless of which slot they used on their pet.

- Crafting a &#160;Saddle grants the Saddle Up! Achievement.

- Equipping a &#160;Textbook to a pet grants the Knowledge is Power! Achievement.
