# Tutorial:Armor Guide

From Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki

Jump to navigation
Jump to search

- This tutorial is a guide.

- This tutorial has been assembled by members of the community as a helpful resource or Tutorial, instructing players on SkyBlock, and thus is not official.

- If you see missing, incorrect or out of date information, please help by contributing to the page.

There are many armors in SkyBlock, but not every single armor is useful. This guide listed the armors you should buy and use in very early to end game.

- For late/end game players and players who have a lot of knowledge about the game, you have the ability to select the most suitable armor for yourself!

## General
[edit | edit source]

Note: if marked with #, then the armor is highly recommended

| Status

| Armor

| Requirements

| Notes

| First Set

| [Farmhand Helmet] [Farmhand Chestplate] [Farmhand Leggings] [Farmhand Boots] 

| None

| The crafting recipe for the Farmhand can be obtained at Wheat&#160;II (2), and is absolutely the first set you should obtain.
#

| [Angler Helmet] [Angler Chestplate] [Angler Leggings] [Angler Boots] 

| None

| Easy to buy from the fish merchant for next to nothing. It can help for players who enjoy fishing, as well as being cheap &#xe021;&#160;Sea Creature Chance for bingo players. Other than the angler equipment, which has no competition at this point in the game, this armor set is usually not worth it if you already have Farmhand Armor.

| Beginner

| [Lapis Armor Helmet] [Lapis Armor Chestplate] [Lapis Armor Leggings] [Lapis Armor Boots] 

| &#160;Mining&#160;V (5)

| This set is nice for new players wishing to level up mining and can be easy to obtain for certain bingo goals, but most players can obtain cheaper armor for earlygame.

| [Rosetta's Helmet] [Rosetta's Chestplate] [Rosetta's Leggings] [Rosetta's Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;III (3)

| The Rosetta's armor set costs 4,530 Coins at [A sprite icon representing Rosetta in Hypixel SkyBlock] Rosetta after obtaining &#160;Combat&#160;III (3), although it is recommended to also obtain &#160;Enchanting&#160;V (5) to gain the full benefits of growth, which can easily be done by enchanting something such as an &#160;Undead Sword.

| [Glacite Helmet] [Glacite Chestplate] [Glacite Leggings] [Glacite Boots] 

| 

| Glacite Armor is the best armor set for beginners, being easy to obtain for both ironman and normal players. Early-game ironman players may find it hard to kill Ice Walkers, but they can be killed in a single hit by a pickaxe. Non-ironman players can easily obtain the armor on the Auction House by saving up some coins.
#

| Early Game

| [Ender Helmet] [Ender Chestplate] [Ender Leggings] [Ender Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;XII (12)

| Ender Armor is the best combat armor for this stage of the game, especially when combined with the &#160;Void Sword, providing for an earlygame powerhouse and easily granting enough damage to kill most creatures on the End Island. It being soulbound means you may have to grind the armor pieces manually, but it is well worth it for 90% of combat players.
#

| Mid-Early Game

| [Old Dragon Helmet] [Old Dragon Chestplate] [Old Dragon Leggings] [Old Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;XVI (16)

| Don't use this armor set unless you plan on playing as tank in Dungeons.

| [Unstable Dragon Helmet] [Unstable Dragon Chestplate] [Unstable Dragon Leggings] [Unstable Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;XVI (16)

| Cheap legendary armor that actually boosts your &#xe050;&#160;Damage. The full set bonus isn't very useful, but the &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage bonuses are extremely helpful for this stage in the game.
#

| Late-Early Game

| [Strong Dragon Helmet] [Tarantula Helmet] [Strong Dragon Chestplate] [Strong Dragon Leggings] [Strong Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;XVI (16)(Spider Slayer&#160;IV (4), if the Tarantula Helmet is used.)

| Unless your primary weapon is still the &#160;Aspect of the End, you should consider switching some armor pieces out for pieces of &#160;Unstable Dragon Armor and/or use a &#160;Tarantula Helmet, which can help balance &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage and result in higher damage overall, or save some coins as they are often a lot cheaper.

| [Crimson Helmet] [Crimson Chestplate] [Crimson Leggings] [Crimson Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;XXII (22) and Basic Tier Kuudra completion

| If you do not plan on doing dungeons, or if you wish to focus on out-of-dungeon combat, it is recommended to acquire this armor set early to slowly upgrade it over the course of your playtime as it is the best melee armor set for out-of-dungeon melee combat for now. However, this armor set is practically useless inside dungeons after the first floor. The helmet should be replaced by a &#160;Tarantula Helmet if you have one for now.
#

| Transition Note

| 

| 

| It is possible to transition straight from Unstable Dragon Armor to Shadow Assassin, via using various sets in dungeons, and possibly some other sets for other game elements, such as slayers and dungeons.

| Early-Mid Game

| [Shadow Assassin Helmet] [Shadow Assassin Chestplate] [Zombie Knight Chestplate] [Shadow Assassin Leggings] [Shadow Assassin Boots] 

| Floor 5 Completion

| With this armor set, the chestplate is not necessary due to its high cost relative to the rest of this armor set. A Legendary &#160;Zombie Knight Chestplate or a &#160;Skeleton Master Chestplate dropped from floor 5 or 6 is a good alternative for those who cannot afford the full set. The &#160;Tarantula Helmet is still a good alternative helmet outside dungeons. Remember to star your armor using &#160;Livid Fragments for additional stats and a rarity upgrade.
#

| Mid Game

| [Tarantula Helmet] [Necron's Chestplate] [Necron's Leggings] [Necron's Boots] 

| Floor VII Completion, Spider Slayer&#160;IV (4)

| This is essential for any player and Berserk class player. As it not only provides great &#xe008;&#160;Defense and &#xe010;&#160;Health, but also a great amount of &#xe050;&#160;Damage. If you can afford it, you can upgrade your &#160;Tarantula Helmet into a &#160;Primordial Helmet at this point, and many may choose to select &#160;Maxor's Boots for additional in-dungeons speed and additional &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage for more balance.
#

| [Frozen Blaze Helmet] [Frozen Blaze Chestplate] [Frozen Blaze Leggings] [Frozen Blaze Boots] 

| &#160;Combat&#160;XXII (22)

| A great alternative to the &#160;Necron's Armor, albeit much more expensive and relies on the &#160;Blaze Pet. However, it is extremely potent as an armor set that has defied power creep since its inception, being viable even over &#160;Burning Crimson Armor for Crimson Isle content. Use this armor if you either prefer the ice aesthetic, have a lot of spare money from non-combat money making methods such as Farming or Mining, or if you want to use its full set bonus for Loot Sharing with Fishing content.

| Later-Mid Game

| [Warden Helmet] [Necron's Chestplate] [Necron's Leggings] [Necron's Boots] 

| Floor VII Completion, Zombie Slayer&#160;VIII (8)

| This is essential for any player and Berserk class player. As it not only provides great &#xe008;&#160;Defense and &#xe010;&#160;Health, but also a great amount of &#xe050;&#160;Damage. The &#160;Warden Helmet, though rather expensive, has the highest damage bonus of any armor piece and is rather important for most late-game combat, especially for the Crimson Isle and high-tier slayers. However, many players may prefer to use the &#160;Primordial Helmet or the &#160;Crown of Avarice over it due to the lack of a speed penalty.
# 

| Late-Game

| [Infernal Crimson Helmet] [Infernal Crimson Chestplate] [Infernal Crimson Leggings] [Infernal Crimson Boots] 

| Infernal Tier Kuudra Completion, Combat Level 47

| This set is the best &#xe050;&#160;Damage set in the game due to the Dominus set ability. It provides very high &#xe010;&#160;Health and high &#xe008;&#160;Defense, and a very high amount of &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage. The Dominus ability grants &#xe024;&#160;Swing Range, at tiers Burning and above gives extra &#xe00b;&#160;Ferocity, and at Infernal tier, gives an additive +10%&#160;&#xe050;&#160;Damage. It is a must-have for any task that requires high damage. However, some players may choose the &#160;Warden Helmet for the similar damage and lower cost compared to an infernal helmet.
# 

| Late-Game

| [Infernal Aurora Helmet] [Infernal Aurora Chestplate] [Infernal Aurora Leggings] [Infernal Aurora Boots] 

| Infernal Tier Kuudra Completion, Combat Level 47

| This set is the best &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence set in the game, giving 1,424&#160;&#xe003;&#160;Intelligence, 274&#160;&#xe003;&#160;Intelligence more than Storm's Armor. It provides very high &#xe010;&#160;Health and high &#xe008;&#160;Defense, along with 40&#160;&#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage. The set bonus Arcane Energy gives the wearer the ability to gain Arcane Energy, and at 10 Arcane Energy, they can sneak to fire homing missiles, firing more the higher tier the armor is. It is a must-have for any task that requires &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence or &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage. The &#160;Wither Goggles is a less expensive alternative helmet that provides similar &#xe002;&#160;Ability Damage at a much lower cost.

| Armor

| Notes

| [Unstable Dragon Helmet] [Unstable Dragon Chestplate] [Unstable Dragon Leggings] [Unstable Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat 16

| The Unstable Dragon Armor set provide a decent balance of &#xe010;&#160;Health, &#xe008;&#160;Defense and &#xe050;&#160;Damage, making it viable for lower floors, (and outside of dungeons) such as The Entrance, and Floor I. Its low requirements, being only 1 &#160;Combat level higher then the requirements for entering dungeons, combined with its low price makes it a great option for starting off.

| [Unstable Dragon Helmet] [Unstable Dragon Chestplate] [Unstable Dragon Leggings] [Unstable Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat 16

| See above

| [Strong Dragon Helmet] [Strong Dragon Chestplate] [Strong Dragon Leggings] [Strong Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat 16

| This armor set has slightly higher damage output than &#160;Unstable Dragon Armor, and is generally a better pick.

| Floor III

| [Superior Dragon Helmet] [Superior Dragon Chestplate] [Superior Dragon Leggings] [Superior Dragon Boots] 

| &#160;Combat 20

| You are not affording this armor set for floor 3. If you can afford it, buy carries up to floor 5 and use &#160;Shadow Assassin Armor instead.

| [Adaptive Helmet] [Adaptive Chestplate] [Adaptive Leggings] [Adaptive Boots] 

| Floor III Completion

| Full &#160;Adaptive Armor, along with the &#160;Adaptive Belt, are a great option for every class. Using &#160;Scarf Fragments to star the armor is recommended.

| Floor IV

| [Adaptive Helmet] [Adaptive Chestplate] [Adaptive Leggings] [Adaptive Boots] 

| Floor III Completion

| See above

| Floor V & Floor VI

| [Shadow Assassin Helmet] [Shadow Assassin Chestplate] [Zombie Knight Chestplate] [Skeleton Master Chestplate] [Shadow Assassin Leggings] [Shadow Assassin Boots] 

| Floor V Completion

| Remember to star your armor using &#160;Livid Fragments for additional stats and a rarity upgrade. A high quality &#160;Zombie Knight Chestplate or &#160;Skeleton Master Chestplate can be used as a budget alternative to the expensive chestplate for Berserk and Archer players respectively.

| [Wither Goggles] [Reaper Mask] [Necromancer Lord Chestplate] [Necromancer Lord Leggings] [Necromancer Lord Boots] 

| Floor VI Completion

| Necromancer Lord Armor is a great armor set for Mages and Tanks for this stage. The &#160;Necromancer Lord Helmet should not be used at all due to better alternatives for both classes. Mages should choose the &#160;Wither Goggles, while tanks should go for a &#160;Reaper Mask to boost their survivability.

| Floor VII

| [Golden Necron Head] [Wither Goggles] [Reaper Mask] [Necron's Chestplate] [Storm's Chestplate] [Goldor's Chestplate] [Necron's Leggings] [Storm's Leggings] [Goldor's Leggings] [Necron's Boots] [Storm's Boots] [Goldor's Boots] 

| Floor VII Completion

| The recommended armor sets provide the greatest amount of &#xe010;&#160;Health, &#xe008;&#160;Defense, &#xe050;&#160;Damage and &#xe003;&#160;Intelligence out of all the Dungeon Armor sets. &#160;Necron's Armor is best for Berserk and Archer players, &#160;Storm's Armor is best for Mage players, and &#160;Goldor's Armor is best for Tank players. Due to the lackluster full set bonus, it is recommended to replace the helmet with either &#160;Wither Goggles for &#160;Storm's Armor, &#160;Reaper Mask for &#160;Goldor's Armor, or &#160;Primordial Helmet or &#160;Golden Necron Head if it's &#160;Necron's Armor. For both Mages and Tanks, the boots can be replaced by Tier 13 &#160;Perfect Boots for additional &#xe008;&#160;Defense.
