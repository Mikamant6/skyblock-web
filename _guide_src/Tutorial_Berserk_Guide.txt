# Tutorial:Berserk Guide

From Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki

Jump to navigation
Jump to search

- This tutorial is a guide.

- This tutorial has been assembled by members of the community as a helpful resource or Tutorial, instructing players on SkyBlock, and thus is not official.

- If you see missing, incorrect or out of date information, please help by contributing to the page.

Berserk is a class in Dungeons mostly centered around melee &#xe050;&#160;Damage. Berserkers rely heavily on &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage for damage. Whether you are playing Dungeons or Slayers, this tutorial can teach you how to become an efficient Berserker.

Most of this tips also apply to Left-Click Mage, a sub-class of Mage specializing on using the mage beam as a primary form of dealing damage.

Before you read this guide, it is important to note Magical Power. Arguably the most important and often overlooked stat in the game, you should have around 300 between Floors 1-3, 550 by Floors 5 and 6, 700 by Floor 7, 950 by Master Mode F3, 1200 by Master Mode F5, and at least 1500 by Master Mode F7. You increase your Magical Power by having Accessories in your Accessory Bag, and harness it by using Accessory Powers.

If you plan on using a [Terminator] &#160;Terminator in Floor VII, you should have at least 1000 Magical Power.

## Tips
[edit | edit source]

- The most optimal way to play Berserk in Master Mode is to wear a Golden Head or a Diamond Head of that floor with [Necron's Helmet] &#160;Necron's Armor, a [Golden Dragon Pet] &#160;Golden Dragon Pet, and a [Terminator] &#160;Terminator, as melee doesn't really work well in Master Mode.

- The [Tarantula Helmet] &#160;Tarantula Helmet's ability does not scale in dungeons and is therefore not very good in mid-game or above.

- The [Warden Helmet] &#160;Warden Helmet halves your speed which is not beneficial for clearing, so it should be avoided if one wants to clear efficiently. It also cannot be dungeonized, resulting in it often being worse than most alternatives.
