## Key

[O] - Optional Step

[OR] Optional Step, but recommended.

[ST] A money-making method or strategy.

[IM] A very important and very recommended step.

## Early Game
[edit | edit source]

- Leave your island. You can do /hub, use the Hub Portal, or bridge over if you are starting completely fresh.

- Buy a &#160;Promising Pickaxe, &#160;Rookie Hoe, and a Golden Axe. You can get the Promising Pickaxe from the Mine Merchant, the Rookie Hoe from the Farm Merchant, and the Golden Axe by crafting it with 2 sticks and 3 gold from the Mine Merchant.

- [O] Start playing the Chocolate Factory. You can open the menu with /factory, /cf, or /chocolatefactory. It is mostly passive progression, so it is worth starting early.

- [IM] Once you get to SkyBlock Level 3, go to the Community Center and get some Account & Profile Upgrades from Elizabeth. It is recommended to prioritize Accessory Bag slots, Minion slots, and Ender Chest Pages.

- Level up your early skills and collections to get to SkyBlock Level 7. This unlocks the Bazaar, which is one of the most important early-game unlocks. Good early methods include mining sand, farming wheat, chopping trees in The Park, and killing early combat mobs.

- Do some NPC Flipping if the Bazaar prices make it profitable. The general idea is to buy items from NPCs and sell them to the Bazaar. Some commonly checked items include &#160;Sand, &#160;Ice, &#160;Packed Ice, &#160;Dandelion, &#160;Flint, &#160;Iron Ingot, &#160;String, &#160;Gunpowder, and &#160;Slimeballs. Always check Bazaar prices first, because these flips change often.

- [ST] A good early game money making method is farming &#160;Glowing Mushrooms and using Chum Buckets. Go to the Glowing Mushroom Cave, talk to [A sprite icon representing Moby in Hypixel SkyBlock] Moby, complete his early quests, and buy &#160;Moby's Shears when possible. Farm Glowing Mushrooms, then check the Bazaar prices of Empty Chum Buckets and Full Chum Buckets before selling.

- [ST] Another good early method after unlocking The End is mining Ender Nodes. This can give experience bottles, Ender equipment, mite drops, and other useful items. It is simple, cheap, and beginner-friendly.

- One of the best armor sets for early game is &#160;Mushroom Armor, as it gives tripled stats at night. You can also use &#160;Hardened Diamond Armor or &#160;Glacite Armor. Do not spend too much money on very early-game armor, because it will be replaced quickly.

- Once you unlock the Dwarven Mines, you can grind for &#160;Glacite Armor or purchase it from the Auction House. It gives good &#xe008;&#160;Defense and is useful for early survivability.

- You could get the Foraging Set from the Into the Woods questline from Charlie and other NPCs in The Park.

- For early combat, use a cheap starter weapon until you can get a &#160;Void Sword or an &#160;Aspect of the End. The &#160;Void Sword is very good in The End when paired with Ender Armor and Ender equipment.

- [IM] Get an Aspect of the End as soon as you reasonably can. Its teleport ability remains useful throughout the entire game, even after the damage becomes irrelevant.

- Get some more minion slots by crafting unique minions and upgrading current minions. You should try to get at least 7 minion slots early.

- Talk to Maxwell and select a power. Fortuitous is a good beginner option because it gives a balance of &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage.

- Get some Redstone Minions for your Redstone Collection, which gives Accessory Bag slots. Accessory Bag slots are very important because they let you increase your Accessory Power. Put Compactors in your Redstone Minions when possible.

- [O] You could get one Gold Minion to help with Bank upgrades, since gold collection can matter later.

- [O] You could also get one Emerald Minion for bank-related collection requirements, though this is less important than Redstone or Gold early on.

- Talk to the Redstone Miner and give him 1,920 Redstone in total for 4 extra accessory slots.

- Upgrade your pickaxe to a &#160;Lapis Pickaxe or another cheap early pickaxe.

- Work on easy talismans. Buy the cheap ones from the Combat Merchant and collect easy ones from early collections, quests, and events.

- Upgrade your current axe to a &#160;Jungle Axe or &#160;Treecapitator when you can afford it. These are useful for Foraging.

- Events are very good for early game money because many of them require little gear to participate in.

- You can do Diana's Mythological Ritual event when she is mayor. Buy a Common Griffin Pet and Ancestral Spade, then look for Griffin Burrows. Griffin Feathers can be sold on the Bazaar, but always check the current price.

- The Spooky Festival is also useful for money making. Kill spooky mobs, sell useful drops, and check Bazaar prices before deciding what to keep.

- The Season of Jerry event can give good money from gifts and event drops. Participate in the event, kill mobs, and sell useful items on the Bazaar if the prices are good.

- [IM] Get a Wand of Healing. It is one of the most useful early healing items. The best way is usually to get Zombie Slayer I and craft it yourself with &#160;Revenant Flesh and a stick.

- [O] Get a Raider Sword from the Melancholic Viking if you want another early weapon, but this is optional if you are using a &#160;Void Sword or &#160;Aspect of the End.

- [IM] As soon as you unlock the Rift Dimension, start progressing through it. Open the Rift Guide if you are confused. Rift progression gives useful rewards, SkyBlock XP, money-making options, and the very useful &#160;Rift Necklace.

- Rift Time is important when starting out in the Rift. A good early method is to collect Enigma Souls after talking to [A sprite icon representing Enigma in Hypixel SkyBlock] Enigma, because they increase your available Rift Time and make later objectives easier.

- Get &#160;Combat skill to level 12 by killing Crypt Ghouls, Miner Zombies, Miner Skeletons, or other mobs you can kill efficiently. Put your coins in the bank before grinding if you are likely to die.

- Go to The End, talk to the Lone Adventurer, and buy a Void Sword. Then grind Endermen for a full set of Ender Armor and Ender equipment.

- Go to the Hub and reforge your Void Sword to a useful damage reforge. Enchant it with Sharpness and other useful enchantments you have unlocked.

- Return to The End with your Ender Armor equipped.

- Grind Seers in the Dragon's Nest until you obtain an Ender Cloak.

- Kill Obsidian Defenders in the Dragon's Nest until you obtain an Ender Belt.

- Mine Ender Nodes until you obtain an Ender Gauntlet.

- Reforge your Ender Equipment to a useful equipment reforge. Menacing and Blended are good general options. Brilliant can be used if you are planning to play Mage.

- Continue the Lone Adventurer questline and obtain the Dragon Shortbow. It is a very useful early bow.

- [IM] Upgrade your Wand of Healing into a Wand of Mending when possible.

- [OR] Try to get some &#160;Fairy Souls when convenient. Fairy Souls no longer give direct stats, but the backpack slots and completion rewards are still useful.

- [IM] Start working on The Garden. It helps with Farming levels, Garden levels, crop collections, visitors, pests, copper, and money. Mushrooms are good for Farming XP, and Melons are often good for money. A Rabbit Pet is useful for Farming XP, while &#160;Mooshroom Cow Pet and &#160;Elephant Pet are useful for farming-related stats and coins.

- [OR] Start doing basic Commissions in the Dwarven Mines. Mining takes a long time to become profitable, but early Heart of the Mountain progression is useful later.

- [OR] Start progressing through Galatea after doing the required Park quests. Galatea introduces modern Foraging and Hunting progression. Do not rush expensive Attributes too early, but learning the system and collecting useful shards over time is recommended.

## Early Mid Game
[edit | edit source]

- [IM] Start to AH flip, Bazaar flip, farm, mine, run Dungeons, or use another money-making method if you have not started already. You will need a stable source of coins for better gear.

- Continue working on The Garden. Focus on Crop Milestones, Garden levels, Visitors, Copper, and better farming tools. The Garden is one of the most useful early-mid game systems.

- [IM] Get better armor, such as &#160;Strong Dragon Armor, &#160;Unstable Dragon Armor, &#160;Young Dragon Armor, or &#160;Wise Dragon Armor, depending on what you are doing. Young Dragon is useful for speed, Strong and Unstable are useful for damage, and Wise is useful for early Mage.

- Grind upper-level Endermen until you are at least &#160;Combat 18. You can then use an Aspect of the Dragons if you want a stronger early melee weapon. However, do not overinvest into outdated weapons if you are close to using Dungeon weapons instead.

- A Griffin Pet works well for early stats and &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find. Other useful early pets include Tiger Pet, Lion Pet, Enderman Pet, Rabbit Pet, Sheep Pet, and Blue Whale Pet, depending on your class and activity.

- Start doing Dungeons. Do not only get carried; learn secrets, puzzles, trap rooms, blood rushing, clearing, and boss mechanics. Dungeon knowledge is just as important as gear.

- When doing Dungeons, always have useful Dungeon utility items. Recommended items include &#160;Dungeonbreaker, &#160;Spirit Leap, &#160;Superboom TNT, &#160;Dungeon Sack, &#160;Inflatable Jerry, and mobility items such as &#160;Bonzo's Staff. Dungeonbreaker is especially useful for breaking through many Catacombs walls and speeding up secret routes.

- [O] If you want a decent early Dungeon bow, get the &#160;Spirit Shortbow and frag it when possible. It is not required for every class, but it is a useful early ranged weapon.

- [IM] Once you get &#160;Combat 22 and can access the Crimson Isle, start doing The Matriarch when possible. &#160;Heavy Pearls are time-gated and are needed later for Crimson Isle armor upgrades.

- Replace your &#160;Ender Belt with a better belt when possible. Good options include &#160;Adaptive Belt, &#160;Molten Belt, or other equipment depending on your budget and activity.

- If you have &#160;Combat 20, buy 9 Beating Hearts from the Bazaar and unlock the Bloody power at Maxwell. It is a good cheap melee power. Tank players can consider defensive powers, and Mage players can consider Intelligence-focused powers.

- [IM] Get a better mid-game weapon. Good options include &#160;Livid Dagger, &#160;Bouquet of Lies, &#160;Felthorn Reaper, &#160;Spirit Sceptre, or &#160;Juju Shortbow, depending on your class. Do not assume one weapon is best for every situation.

- [IM] Upgrade your armor to &#160;Shadow Assassin Armor for melee/archer progression, &#160;Wise Dragon Armor or Dungeonized Mage armor for Mage progression, or tankier Dungeon armor if you are playing Tank. You can use a Zombie Knight Chestplate as a budget replacement for Shadow Assassin Chestplate, but make sure you meet its Catacombs requirement.

- Replace your &#160;Ender Cloak with a better cloak when possible. Good early-mid options include &#160;Shadow Assassin Cloak, &#160;Molten Cloak, &#160;Rift Necklace, or other equipment depending on your activity.

- [ST] Dungeons can make money, but do not rely on fixed profit numbers. Chest prices and item values change often. Always check current prices before opening expensive chests.

- Focus on more talismans and minions for passive money and Accessory Power. Try to get at least 25% accessory completion and 12 or more minion slots by this stage.

- [ST] Use a Minion Spreadsheet or current community resources to find profitable minions. However, collection minions are still useful because they unlock recipes, upgrades, and profile progression.

- Start doing Slayer Quests. [A sprite icon representing Maddox the Slayer in Hypixel SkyBlock] Maddox the Slayer is underneath the Tavern.

- Replace your &#160;Ender Necklace with a better necklace when possible. &#160;Rift Necklace is excellent if you have progressed the Rift. &#160;Molten Necklace is also useful for Crimson Isle and combat.

- [O] The player can optionally get more Fairy Souls for backpack slots and completion.

- Start progressing through Elle's questline on the Crimson Isle when you can survive there. This unlocks important Crimson Isle progression and later Kuudra.

- Do not only focus on Dungeons and Slayer. You can also progress Collections, Bestiary, The Garden, Rift, Mining, Foraging, Hunting, and SkyBlock Levels.

Now that you have a stable early money-making method, basic Dungeon progress, some Slayer progress, Garden progress, and better gear, you are entering:

## Mid Game
[edit | edit source]

- [IM] Build a reliable money-making method. Good options include Farming, Mining preparation, Dungeons, Bazaar flipping, Auction flipping, Rift methods, event grinding, and later Kuudra. Choose something you are willing to do for a long time.

- You may have started Mining earlier, but Mining becomes much stronger after more Heart of the Mountain progress, Powder, better gear, and Crystal Hollows access. Continue doing commissions and work toward better mining gear over time.

- [IM] Continue The Garden. Work on Crop Milestones, Visitors, Copper upgrades, farming tools, and better Farming Fortune. The Garden is one of the most reliable progression systems for mid-game players.

- [IM] Continue Rift progression. Work on Timecharms, Rift Necklace, SkyBlock XP, and Rift money-making methods.

- [IM] Start doing [A sprite icon representing Elle in Hypixel SkyBlock] Elle's questline if you have not already. Unlocking Kuudra and Crimson Isle progression is very important later.

- [IM] Do Enderman Slayer until you reach Enderman Slayer V if you want to use a &#160;Juju Shortbow. Juju Shortbow is a major mid-game Archer weapon.

- Upgrade your Wand of Mending into a Wand of Restoration when possible. This requires Zombie Slayer VI.

- Get better Pets, such as Blue Whale Pet for survivability and Tank, Baby Yeti Pet for Strength-based survivability, Sheep Pet for Mage, Tiger Pet or Lion Pet for Berserk, Skeleton Pet for Archer, and Wither Skeleton Pet for many Dungeon situations.

- Do Dungeons often enough to complete Floor V, Floor VI, and eventually Floor VII. Learn secrets and routes instead of only relying on carries.

- Get stronger class armor. Common routes include &#160;Shadow Assassin Armor into &#160;Necron's Armor, early Mage gear into &#160;Storm's Armor, and tank armor into &#160;Goldor's Armor.

- [IM] Get one or more strong mid-game weapons depending on your class: &#160;Bouquet of Lies, &#160;Felthorn Reaper, &#160;Livid Dagger, &#160;Spirit Sceptre, &#160;Juju Shortbow, or later &#160;Giant's Sword.

- Get Wolf Slayer VI and a Mana Flux Power Orb. Later, upgrade to Overflux Power Orb when possible.

- Reach at least around 50% Accessory completion over time. Recombobulating important high-rarity accessories can be useful later, but do not waste coins before your basic Accessory Power is fixed.

- Get your Slayer levels higher. Useful goals include Zombie Slayer VI or VII, Wolf Slayer VI or VII, Spider Slayer progression for accessories, and Enderman Slayer V for Juju Shortbow.

- Your skill average should continue improving. Do not ignore skills such as Farming, Mining, Enchanting, Combat, and Alchemy.

- Upgrade your Wand of Restoration into a Wand of Atonement when you can afford it and meet the Zombie Slayer requirement.

- Progress the Crimson Isle more. Start collecting &#160;Heavy Pearls from The Matriarch regularly, because they are time-gated and needed for later armor upgrades.

- [OR] Start learning Hunting and Attributes in Galatea. Attributes are powerful, but expensive upgrades should usually wait until you know which ones you actually need.

## Late Game
[edit | edit source]

- Max collections one by one using collection minions, manual grinding, or other efficient methods.

- Do Kuudra for coins, Crimson Essence, armor progression, attributes, and Crimson Isle progression. Kuudra becomes more important as your gear improves.

- Get and upgrade the Kuudra armor variants you need. Crimson Armor is commonly used for melee, Aurora Armor for Mage, Terror Armor for Archer, Fervor Armor for defensive uses, and Hollow Armor for support-related uses.

- Get the Wither Armor variants that match your class if you have not already. &#160;Necron's Armor is used for Berserk and Archer, &#160;Storm's Armor for Mage, &#160;Goldor's Armor for Tank, and &#160;Maxor's Armor for speed and clearing utility.

- [IM] Work toward a fully scrolled Necron's Blade variant if you are playing Mage or need high-end utility. A &#160;Hyperion with all three Necron's Blade Scrolls is the main goal because applying all three scrolls gives Wither Impact.

- Replace your older equipment with stronger equipment depending on your class and activity. Examples include &#160;Implosion Belt, &#160;Gauntlet of Contagion, File:Molten Equipment.png&#160;Molten Equipment, Rift equipment, Dungeon equipment, or Kuudra equipment.

- Get all important Slayers to level VIII or IX over time. Zombie Slayer VIII is useful for its health regeneration perk, and Enderman Slayer VII is required for Terminator.

- Upgrade your bow into a Terminator once you have Enderman Slayer VII and enough Accessory Power, Crit Chance, gear, and money to use it properly. Terminator is extremely strong, but it requires proper setup.

- Continue improving your main money-making method. Late-game upgrades are expensive, so stable income matters more than short bursts of profit.

- Start serious Master Mode progression when your Catacombs level, gear, Accessory Power, pets, and class knowledge are high enough.

- Work on high-tier Attributes, Kuudra armor upgrades, SkyBlock Levels, pets, accessories, collections, and bestiary.

## End Game
[edit | edit source]

- Reach very high SkyBlock Levels.

- Max or nearly max your skills.

- Max or nearly max your accessories and Accessory Power.

- Grind Master Catacombs and work toward The Catacombs - Master Mode Floor VII.

- Get a Dark Claymore.

- Own and optimize major high-end weapons such as &#160;Hyperion, Terminator, and Dark Claymore.

- Own and optimize important pets such as Golden Dragon Pet, Ender Dragon Pet, Black Cat Pet, class pets, and skill pets.

- Max important Slayer levels.

- Progress high-tier Kuudra.

- Upgrade Kuudra armor sets and important Attributes.

- Complete or nearly complete The Garden, Rift, Mining, Bestiary, Collections, Museum, Pet Score, and other long-term systems.

- Flex with your damage, your profile, your museum, or your terrible financial decisions.

- Touch some grass. It is the green stuff outside, sort of like the one that appears when you leave dirt alone for a few minutes.

Nice, now you can finally start a new profile and do these things all over again. Just remember to touch grass occasionally.

## Final Tips
[edit | edit source]

- SkyBlock changes often. Always check current Bazaar and Auction House prices before following a money-making method.

- Do not blindly follow old fixed-profit claims. Dungeon chest profits, Bazaar flips, event drops, and item prices change constantly.

- Accessory Power is extremely important. Many players buy better weapons too early while ignoring accessories.

- Do time-gated progression early. Examples include The Garden, Chocolate Factory, Commissions, Experimentation Table, The Matriarch, Rift charges, and minion collection.

- Choose one main money-making method and improve it instead of constantly switching before anything becomes profitable.

- Use the in-game SkyBlock Guide for general direction, but do not follow it blindly. Your best route depends on your class, goals, money-making method, and gear.

- Most importantly, these are not strict rules for how to play the game. The main purpose of video games is having fun. If building your island, collecting items, fishing, farming, mining, doing Dungeons, or experimenting with overpriced items is what you enjoy, then do that.

- Always remember that this is your journey. Do not let other players bully you just because your skill average, networth, Catacombs level, or Accessory Power is lower than theirs.

For more tips, check Tutorials/Tips and Tricks.
