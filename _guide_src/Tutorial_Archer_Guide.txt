# Tutorial:Archer Guide

From Hypixel SkyBlock Wiki

Jump to navigation
Jump to search

- This tutorial is a guide.

- This tutorial has been assembled by members of the community as a helpful resource or Tutorial, instructing players on SkyBlock, and thus is not official.

- If you see missing, incorrect or out of date information, please help by contributing to the page.

The Archer class is a class within Dungeons that focuses on Bows damage. This class depends heavily on &#xe00d;&#160;Strength, &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, like the Berserk class. As a result, both classes play very similarly.

It is worthy to note that it is much more recommended to play Berserk until one obtains a powerful bow like the [Juju Shortbow] &#160;Juju Shortbow, or [Terminator] &#160;Terminator. It is also not recommended to play this if one doesn't have a decent money-making method, as setups can get very expensive on later floors, especially in Master Mode.

## Recommended Weapons
[edit | edit source]

These are heavily recommended items/weapons to use when playing as the Archer class. The best of these often have requirements such as a high &#160;Combat, Slayer, or &#160;Catacombs level. Archer setups are very expensive by nature, and thus the best items will often cost and be worth tens, if not hundreds of millions of coins.

An important note is that most regular Bows that have a drawing time and do not fire instantly, which will be referred to as Longbows, will be almost entirely irrelevant.

### Enchantments
[edit | edit source]

Given that dealing significant &#xe050;&#160;Damage with bows is regularly much harder than dealing large amounts of &#xe050;&#160;Damage with a melee weapon, the Enchantments that an Archer puts on their weapons is extremely important.

| Enchantment
| Notes

| Bows

| Power

| This is a vital and required Enchantment for all Archers. It grants up to +65%&#160;&#xe050;&#160;Damage on your bows.

| Snipe

| This is useful for when you are trying to hit enemies from far away, since it grants increased damage depending on the distance between you and the target. It is useful when the enemy you are trying to hit is very far away or has a large hitbox.

| Piercing

| This is great for clearing, allowing arrows to pass through mobs and deal additional damage to a lesser degree. It pairs well with the Bouncy Arrows passive.

| Infinite Quiver

| This is useful for saving arrows. It's surprisingly cheap, and will be vital as Archers often use a lot of arrows.

| Overload

| This is absolutely vital and required for all Archers. It grants base &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage. Additionally, depending on your &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance, it has a chance for you to deal a Mega Critical Hit, which multiplies your damage by 1.5x. When paired with a [Terminator] &#160;Terminator, which requires players to have high &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance due to it dividing your &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance by 4, this Enchantment will be invaluable.

| Armor

| Rejuvenate

| Grants &#xe011;&#160;Health Regen. Improves survivablility.

| Strong Vitality

| Grants &#xe00d;&#160;Strength.

A good Ultimate Enchantment is also vital for efficient Archer progression.

| Enchantment
| Notes

| Bows

| Soul Eater

| Soul Eater is a recommended Ultimate Enchantment that is excellent for clearing rooms, as its bonus of adding the damage of the last mob killed and applying it onto your next hit: with high enough &#xe050;&#160;Damage, &#xe00d;&#160;Strength, and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, you could clear rooms very efficiently.

| Duplex

| Duplex is a recommended Ultimate Enchantment that is excellent for DPS, allowing your bow to shoot an extra arrow dealing a percentage of the first arrow's damage and dealing increased damage over time. It is not very efficient for clearing, but is recommended to use during boss fights: as a result, it is better to have two bows, one for clearing and one for DPS. This contributes to the Archer class being very expensive.

| Armor

| Legion

| Legion is the best Ultimate Enchantment for Armor, granting increased Combat Stats and &#xe01a;&#160;Magic Find per player near you, which is vital for stats like &#xe00d;&#160;Strength, &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage, and &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance.

### Accessory Powers
[edit | edit source]

A proper Accessory Power and high Magical Power is important to ensure optimum survivability and damage capabilities. Tuning Points should be tuned towards &#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed, although you can spend them on &#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance until you can reach 100%&#160;&#xe02c;&#160;Crit Chance with a [Terminator] &#160;Terminator.

| Slot
| Name

Itchy

| The Itchy Power grants significantly more &#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed than the aforementioned Bloody and Silky Powers, though it lacks &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage. You should aim to balance your &#xe00d;&#160;Strength and &#xe007;&#160;Crit Damage as evenly as possible, so it is good to mix and match to see which builds work for you.
