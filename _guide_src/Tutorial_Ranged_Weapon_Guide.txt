Ranged Weapons are a big part of SkyBlock. Ender Dragons, the Magma Boss, and even Endstone Protectors to some extent, are all mainly defeated using Bows. Additionally, the Archer class is focused around using Bows.

## Target Practice
[edit | edit source]

Main page: Archery Range §&#160;Target Practice

It is highly recommended to complete Target Practice in order to unlock better Arrows.

Usage of a Shortbow is recommended, especially with high &#xe001;&#160;Attack Speed

## Early Game
[edit | edit source]

Many ranged weapons are quite expensive, heavily limiting effective early-game options.

One of the best bows for the early game is the Dragon Shortbow. Until then, an Artisanal Shortbow or an Undead Bow should be used, if a bow is desired.

## Mid Game
[edit | edit source]

There are several bows you can get during Mid Game:

- Last Breath is a bow that reduces &#xe008;&#160;Defense which makes it a strong bow when fighting mobs with high defense (e.g Lost Adventurer, Tank Zombie and Super Tank Zombie)

- The Juju Shortbow is very useful in Dungeons because it has no drawback time, making it one of the most recommended bows during mid game. Also, it is recommended to apply the Soul Eater Enchantment due to the fact that it instantly shoots arrows.

- Spider Queen's Stinger is a great bow with the normal version costing about 300,000 Coins and when starred, 500,000 Coins to 850,000 Coins and can shoot Enderman.

- Venom's Touch does moderate damage, and it's ability is decent too.

- Bonemerang used to be the strongest bow until sword swapping was patched. Its only use at the moment is to stun Thorn when you do it rapidly.

- Souls Rebound does a lot of damage and it's stacking ability is good for dungeon bosses.

## Late Game
[edit | edit source]

- The Mosquito Bow is slightly stronger than the Runaan's Bow during Dragon Fights. It is also the only bow that players can rely on to consistently one-shot Wolves in the Howling Cave, even when they don't charge the arrow all the way.

- Death Bow is a strong bow in dungeons due to it dealing 100% more &#xe050;&#160;Damage to undead mobs. It also has a 50% chance to bounce to another enemy after hitting an enemy. When used with Bouncy Arrows, the bounce rate will turn to 100% chance.

## End Game
[edit | edit source]

- Currently, the only end game bow is the Terminator which is unlocked with Enderman Slayer 7.

## Shortbows
[edit | edit source]

The game has numerous shortbows: a type of bow that fires instantly without needing to recharge. This section lists the types of Shortbows ranging from early to endgame

- Artisanal Shortbow is likely the first shortbow you'll obtain, being purchased from the Weaponsmith for cheap.

- Dragon Shortbow is obtained after finishing the Lost Adventurer's quest. It comes pre-enchanted with Dragon Tracer, making it ideal to leech fragments off Dragon fights.

- Machine Gun Shortbow is the first dungeon shortbow you'll come across. while inside dungeons, it comes with a left-click ability that rapidly shoots arrows.

- Spirit Shortbow is a bow you can obtain at Floor IV of the Catacombs. it deals more damage the lower your health becomes and it can be used while dead on Dungeons.

- Juju Shortbow is a very powerful bow that has high stats and can damage Enderman.

- Terminator is currently the most powerful shortbow in the game, requiring Enderman Slayer Level 7 and a Pray RNGesus drop from the Enderman Slayer. It can shoot 3 arrows and has an ability that deals arrow damage to all enemies hit.
